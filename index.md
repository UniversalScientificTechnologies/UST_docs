## Welcome to UST documentation page

This site documents instruments developed by [Universal Scientific Technologies s.r.o.](https://www.ust.cz/) for atmospheric and electromagnetic research. The instruments cover the full chain from lightning detection and electric-field sensing to integrated mobile and airborne measurement platforms.

### What you can find here?

* **[Radio Storm Monitoring Stations (RSMS)](/RSMS/)** — VLF and UHF SDR receivers for lightning observation, including a [distributed VLF lightning mapping algorithm](/RSMS/VLF-mapping/) and the [lightning trigger theory](/RSMS/lightning-trigger/).
* **[Electric Field Sensors (THUNDERMILL)](/THUNDERMILL/)** — angular-sensitive electric field mills for stationary, mobile and UAV use.
* **[Precipitation Disdrometer (DISDROMETER01)](/DISDROMETER/)** — piezoelectric, waveform-level disdrometer for hydrometeor-type classification on moving platforms.
* **[Storm-resistant Anemometer (WINDGAUGE03)](/WINDGAUGE/)** — Venturi-based, hail-tolerant anemometer with absolute heading via magnetometer.
* **[Storm measurement vehicles](/StormCar/)** — integrated mobile platform combining the above instruments plus an autogyro-launch capability for in-situ thunderstorm measurements.
* **[Solar Patrol telescope](/SolarPatrol/)** — semi-automated solar observation assembly at Ondřejov.
* **[UST ionizing radiation sensors](https://docs.dos.ust.cz/)** — the GEODOS / AIRDOS / SPACEDOS / LABDOS family of radiation detectors (separate site).
